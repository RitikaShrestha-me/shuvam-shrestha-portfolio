My story

CHAPTER 01

I started with business. Curiosity took me to technology.

My career began in bancassurance marketing at one of Nepal's reputed insurance companies, where I worked with corporate banks and business houses. The experience gave me an early understanding of how businesses operate, how relationships shape markets, and how products need to be positioned to create value. While pursuing my MBA, I began looking beyond traditional business strategies. I became increasingly interested in a question: How can data and technology help businesses make better decisions, build better products, and stay competitive? I started exploring on my own through online courses, YouTube, and conversations with people working in technology. What began as curiosity gradually became a career shift. I moved into the IT industry, where my journey in project management introduced me to software development, cross-functional collaboration, and the process of turning business requirements into working solutions. The more I learned about technology, the more questions I wanted to explore. I realised that I didn't just want to manage the development of products; I wanted to understand the data, systems, and intelligence behind them.

CHAPTER 02

From managing projects to understanding data

That curiosity led me to pursue data science at the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT) in Thailand, where I began building my technical foundation in data science, machine learning, data engineering, and analytics. Through the dual-degree opportunity, I continued this journey at Télécom SudParis, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, in France, pursuing an MSc in Data Science and Network Intelligence. Moving across disciplines and countries has challenged me to approach problems differently. My background in business and finance helps me think about value, strategy, and practical outcomes, while my technical studies allow me to explore how data and intelligent systems can help create them.

AIT, Thailand · Aug 2025 – present Télécom SudParis, France · Sep 2026 – present MBA Finance · Purbanchal University

CHAPTER 03

What I am working towards today

I am interested in the space where business problems meet data, AI, and digital product development. I enjoy exploring how data pipelines, machine learning, automation, and AI agents can move beyond experimentation to solve practical problems. I bring a perspective shaped by both sides: understanding why a business needs a solution and learning how to build the technology that can deliver it. I'm still learning, building, and testing ideas. For me, the journey has never been about moving away from my original background. It has been about adding new ways of thinking and new tools to it.

I want to build technology that makes business decisions smarter, products more useful, and complex problems easier to solve.
